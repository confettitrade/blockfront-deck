What

BFT: the token that keeps the network honest

Customers keep paying in USDC. BFT is what everyone who runs, publishes or vouches for data puts on the line.

Stake to run a node

Operators stake BFT to serve traffic and get paid for it. Stale or wrong answers cost them stake.

Stake to publish data

Listing a transformer takes a BFT stake, so junk costs money to post.

Curate and earn

Back the transformers you trust with BFT and share in the query fees they bring in.

Settle disputes

When nodes disagree, the fingerprints decide, and the side that was wrong is slashed.

BFT is not live yet. Design draft; final terms with counsel.

More apps mean more queries. More queries mean more fees to the people staking BFT, more stake needed to serve the traffic, and more BFT burned. Demand for the token grows with real use.

BFT is not live. Design draft, not an offer of tokens. Precedent: The Graph burns 1% of query fees.
