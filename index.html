THE PROBLEM

Building on HyperEVM means trusting someone else’s endpoint

Rate limits

Hyperliquid’s public RPC refused 15 calls in a row as “rate limited”, and caps a log search at 1,000 blocks.

Two numberings

Public endpoints number events two ways. The same confetti trade is event 0 on one and event 1 on another.

No proof

Nothing says an answer is stale. Confetti’s keeper once ran 11 hours on a lagging node without knowing.

Measured 3 November against rpc.hyperliquid.xyz, purroof and drpc

DAY ONE

The fingerprints caught a real data bug on day one.

What

Our two nodes disagreed on confetti’s tables in 111 of 10,833 blocks.

Why

Public endpoints number events two ways, and the nodes had read from both.

Fixed the same day

One numbering, Hyperliquid’s own. Both nodes rebuilt 1.9 million blocks and now match.
